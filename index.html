the project's concept


this project is born initially out of an idea regarding the term "Metamorphisan Legacy" -- tied to the 2013 doujin Metamorphosis / Henshin / Emergence / 177013 by Shindo L. this was supposed to be aimed at 'revitalizing' Yoshida Saki's story and give her the happiest ending that she deserved for so long.


however, the change of shift is due to the fact that multiple titles varying different genres often lead to a related outcome.


through its creative prowess, this project has updated its mission: to offer real-world solutions on issues surrounding womanhood through creative means.


the story so far


following her departure from the manga world, Sumiyoi Sakura -- primarily known as the Princess of the Sumiyoi Clan who ruled the northern portion of a city in Japan -- has been tasked with taking care of a group of so-called "Unfortunates" who had their lives in complete disarray, often through sexual upheavals.


originating from a mysterious snail mail containing the profiles of the "Unfortunates" and their last known location, Sakura must try her absolute best to find and nurture the individuals before their lives begin to turn for the worst.


up north across the Hokkaido region, a mysterious campus stands tall; awaiting to receive the pact..
